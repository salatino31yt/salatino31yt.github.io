Shorts Factory

Shorts Factory is a personal tool that runs on its owner's computer. It produces short history videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account or data.

Shorts Factory uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.
