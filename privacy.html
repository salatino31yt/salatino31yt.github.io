Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Shorts Factory ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. The app uses YouTube API Services.

What data the app accesses

The app requests a single Google permission: youtube.upload, which allows it to upload videos to the authorized YouTube channel. It does not read, collect or store any other information from your Google account or YouTube channel.

How data is stored and shared

The authorization token is stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's own YouTube API, and no data is sold or shared with third parties.

Data deletion

The app keeps no user data on any server. To delete the locally stored authorization token, revoke access (see below) or delete the token file from the owner's computer; Google then invalidates it.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube and Google policies

By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
